Hide Specific Products in POS Receipt

This module allows you to hide selected products from appearing on the POS receipt.

How to use:
1. Go to Products.
2. Open any product form.
3. Enable the option "Hide In POS Receipt".
4. Use the product in POS as usual.
5. The product will not be shown on the printed receipt.

This helps keep your receipts clean by hiding internal, complimentary, or non-customer-facing items.
